Support

How can we help?

Wormwell runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and the core game plays offline. Try the answers below, or write to us with the details of what happened.

The best route for any game or purchase issue.wormwell.game@gmail.com

Common questions

How do I steer the worm?

Touch anywhere on the screen and drag the way you want to go. The first touch is only an anchor, so it never jerks the worm. Let go and the worm keeps crawling in the same direction. Swipe sharply and let go while your finger is still moving to lunge; a fast swipe can crack small stones. Controls are explained in Settings → Controls.

How do I play on Mac?

Wormwell is a native Mac app (macOS 15 or later) with the same game as on iPhone and iPad. Drag with the mouse or trackpad to steer, or use the arrow keys (two keys for a diagonal). Hold Space and release it to lunge. Esc pauses, Return presses the main button, and 1 and 2 use quick actions from your backpack. The game keeps its portrait layout in a window you can resize or make full screen.

Why did my run end?

Energy is the only run resource. Digging fresh soil, lunging, climbing and bumping into stone all cost energy; food is the only way to refill it. When energy reaches zero the run ends and your depth is recorded. Upgrades between runs, such as Energy Capacity and Better Digestion, help you go further.

What are camps?

Camps are lantern-lit domes deep underground. Reach a camp and you can start your next run from it: use the arrows on the home screen to choose the meadow or any camp you have opened. Your record always counts the absolute depth.

An Amber purchase did not arrive

Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account you used for the purchase and is online, then reopen the Amber shop and wait a moment: pending transactions are delivered when the App Store confirms them. If the Amber is still missing, use Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Amber shop. If a completed charge was still not delivered, email us the pack name and the approximate time of purchase. Never send full payment-card details.

Game Center scores or achievements are missing

Check that Game Center is signed in under Settings on your device, then reopen Wormwell while online. Achievements and records are always kept in the game itself, even when you are signed out; signing in later sends them to Game Center.

I changed or lost my device

Wormwell keeps your progress on the device. On a new device, progress returns from a device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). Amber packs bought from the App Store can be restored with Restore Purchases in the Amber shop when you are signed in to the same Apple Account.

There is no sound or vibration

Check Music, Sound Effects and Haptics in Wormwell Settings. Also check the device volume, Silent Mode, Focus and whether audio is going to headphones or another device. Macs have no game haptics.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in Wormwell and choose Language. The game is available in 14 languages and switches at once, without restarting.

The game feels too busy or makes me dizzy

Turn on Reduce Motion or Reduced Effects in Wormwell Settings. They calm camera motion and visual effects without changing the game.

What should I include in a bug report?

Your iPhone, iPad or Mac model, the system version, the Wormwell version, the depth or zone where it happened, what you expected, what happened instead, and steps to make it happen again. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information.

App Store billing and refunds

Apple handles App Store billing and refunds. For a charge or a refund request, use Apple’s Report a Problem page. We are happy to help identify the Wormwell product involved, but we cannot see or change your payment method.
