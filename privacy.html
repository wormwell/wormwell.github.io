Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Wormwell, the digging game for iPhone, iPad and Mac (the “app”), and this website handle information. Questions can be sent to wormwell.game@gmail.com.

In short
  • Wormwell has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • There is no Wormwell account and no developer-operated game server.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases and Game Center activity.
  • The developer does not collect any data from the app.

1. Data stored by the app

Wormwell stores your settings and language, depth records, opened camps, upgrades, zone adaptations, equipment, wardrobe items, Amber balance, achievements and statistics, and the identifiers needed to deliver purchases exactly once. This information stays on your device and is used only to run the game and keep your progress.

Deleting the app removes its data from the device, subject to device backups controlled by you and Apple.

2. App Store purchases

Optional Amber packs are sold through Apple’s StoreKit. Apple processes the payment; the developer never receives your payment-card details or Apple Account information. The app receives product and transaction information from Apple only to deliver the Amber you bought and to prevent duplicate delivery. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy and terms.

3. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Wormwell submits your best depth and other leaderboard scores, reports achievements and may show leaderboards. Your Game Center identity and activity are managed by Apple, and you control Game Center in your device settings. The whole game works without Game Center.

4. iCloud and backups

The current version does not sync game progress through iCloud and does not send it to the developer. Apple may include app data in device backups according to your backup settings. If a future version adds optional iCloud sync, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

5. Data the developer collects

The app does not send gameplay, profile, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, diagnostics or usage analytics to the developer. The developer receives personal information only if you choose to email us: your email address, your message and anything you decide to attach. This correspondence is used to answer you, to fix problems in the app and to keep necessary support records.

6. This website

This static website uses no cookies, analytics, advertising, fonts or scripts from third parties, and no forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information such as your IP address and browser details to operate and secure its hosting, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

7. Children

Wormwell does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no account, chat, advertising, analytics or developer server. A parent or guardian who believes a child has emailed us personal information can contact us to have it deleted.

8. Your choices, access and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete the app. Purchase, Game Center and backup data held by Apple is managed through your Apple Account and device settings. Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to answer you, protect the service and meet legal obligations. To access or delete anything you sent us, email wormwell.game@gmail.com.

9. Security and international processing

Data on your device is protected by your device’s security. Email and hosting providers may process information in countries other than your own. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
